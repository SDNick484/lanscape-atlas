How to use this map
  • Zoom: scroll, pinch the trackpad, or use + / −. Fit shows everything.
  • Pan: drag empty space.
  • Move: drag a device (saved automatically).
  • Search: type a name, IP or MAC and pick a result.
  • Connect: shift-click two devices (or drag a box around them), then "Connect selected".
  • Unmanaged switch or hub: "Add placeholder", connect it to its uplink, then connect each device behind it. That device's dashed router line then hides.
  • Delete: click a solid line, then "Delete link". Dashed lines come from the router and can't be deleted.
  • Colours: a device's fill is its room's colour (legend in the toolbar).
